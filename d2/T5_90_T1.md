## 2. The nonconstant-E case has one local shape

Factor backwards as `g6=q^6 G6` and `g5=q^5 G5`. At the root of `s`, put


```text

```

and the level-5 equation has orders


The terminal equation says `2v_s(d1)=3+h`. Exhausting
`h=1,3,5,7,9`, including zero `sigma`, `d2`, `G6`, or `G5`, leaves only


Consequently every nonconstant solution must have


The first nonzero coefficients of levels 5 and 6 give more than just the
orders. Reducing those equations modulo `s`, then using (2), produces


Thus the exceptional root is forced to satisfy


## 3. Constant E is infeasible

Now `E=gamma`, `d1=delta V`, `H=eta V^2`, and put `d=deg V`.
The possibilities are `d=0,...,4` and `sigma=0` or
`deg sigma=0,...,8`: 50 degree cells.

```text

```

while all `f<=4` terms are bounded by `229+3f<=241`. In 43 cells a
single one of `f=5,6,7` is the unique top term, so those cells are
infeasible. The only initial ties are

```text

```
