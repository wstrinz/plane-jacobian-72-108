## Verdict

**κ = t−2 EXTENDS to every escape family — the composition heuristic κ = l₂−l₁ is
dead.** The reason is structural: every transformation in a GGV/GGHV reduction
chain is a root-shift shear (Jacobian 1, bracket-preserving), and the chain makes
exactly **one** final inversion. Fusing the shears into the final map gives


symbolically for generic l and up to three shear terms). The heuristic κ = l₂−l₁
is the Jacobian of a chart with *two* inversions — a chart the chain never
